Rooted in Drive. Dressed in Legacy.

In a fast-moving world, GhViƆɔ (pronounced Ga-vee-chi) invites offroaders to slow down — and find meaning in motion. Our philosophy honors the self we show and the self we hold close — blending quiet strength with refined elegance. Rooted in East African values and shaped by global sustainability, our pieces are timeless statements of purpose and restraint.

This collaboration with The Offroad Collective is for those who don’t just roam — but reconnect. Each GhViƆɔ piece plants roots in the earth, weaving legacy into every stitch.

The Legacy Capsule

A curated collection for intentional drivers, grounded in heritage, utility, and sustainability.

What’s Inside:

  • Heritage Shirt: Earth-dyed organic cotton. Minimalist silhouette. A daily layer of quiet strength.
  • Journey Jacket: Durable, water-resistant. Embroidered with East African detailing. Built for rain and ritual.
  • Rooted Scarf: Handwoven. Naturally pigmented. A wearable link to land and legacy.
  • Legacy Hat: Upcycled canvas. Leather strap. Lightweight and intentional.
  • The Seed Notebook: Recycled paper for slow thoughts, field notes, or future plans.
  • Digital Forest Pass: A unique code connecting you to your role in our shared reforestation story.

Tree Planting, Made Clear

Each capsule supports the planting of 5 native trees in East Africa. We plant in collective phases — beginning with 250 capsules.

Once each phase is completed, we plant trees in partnership with trusted local restoration groups. You’ll receive updates and visuals as your forest grows — with real people, real soil, and real stories.

Your Digital Forest Pass gives you access to our live forest tracker, reforestation updates, and community events.

No inflated claims. No empty promises. Just impact, delivered with care.
